01 / Foundation & Commissioning

Early Life & Military Calling

Origins & Education

Born on April 12, 1983, in Kozhikode, Kerala, and brought up in Tambaram, Chennai. Graduated with a Commerce degree from Madras Christian College before following his military calling.

OTA & Commissioning

Joined the prestigious Officers Training Academy (OTA), Chennai, in 2004. Commissioned as a Lieutenant into the renowned 22nd Battalion of The Rajput Regiment on March 18, 2006.

International Service

Served as an instructor at the Infantry School in Mhow and completed an international United Nations peacekeeping mission with UNIFIL in Lebanon.

Promotion to Major

Promoted to the rank of Major in October 2012, recognized for exceptional tactical aptitude, discipline, and exemplary troop command.

02 / Operational Leadership

44 Rashtriya Rifles & Shopian

Counter-Insurgency Duty

Deputed to 44 Rashtriya Rifles (Victor Force) in Shopian district, South Kashmir—one of the most active operational counter-terror domains.

Troop & Local Trust

Earned deep affection from his men for leading from the front, alongside building genuine rapport and trust with local Kashmiri villagers.

Operational Track Record

  • Planned and executed multiple high-risk cordon and search operations.
  • Successfully neutralized JeM Commander Altaf Baba in a decisive counter-terror operation.
  • Set benchmarks for tactical stealth, speed, and strict prevention of collateral civilian harm.

03 / The Final Mission

Operation Qazipathri (25 April 2014)

Immediate Response

Following threats to election personnel in Shopian, intelligence cornered 3 heavily armed insurgents inside a residential structure in Qazipathri village.

Civilian Evacuation

Major Mukund personally ensured all trapped local civilians were safely evacuated out of the line of fire before commencing the assault.

Heroism Under Fire

Forming a buddy pair with Sepoy Vikram Singh, Major Mukund entered the target house. Despite sustaining three fatal gunshot wounds, he single-handedly crawled forward and neutralized the top commander, completing the objective before succumbing to his injuries.

04 / Immortal Honor

Ashoka Chakra Citation

"Major Mukund Varadarajan displayed conspicuous bravery, swift initiative, and supreme leadership in the face of the enemy. His action ensured the elimination of three top terrorists without any civilian casualties."

Republic Day Honor

Awarded India's highest peacetime gallantry award, the Ashoka Chakra, presented by President Pranab Mukherjee to his wife, Indhu Rebecca Varghese, on Jan 26, 2015.

Eternal Legacy

Honored with a bust at OTA Chennai and immortalized in national memory, including the major 2024 biographical film 'Amaran'.
